About the dashboard

What the report shows

The dashboard rolls a single post-level dataset up into the views that matter to a brand — by platform, region, content format and hashtag — reporting true engagement totals alongside average performance per post, so volume and quality can be read separately.

  • Four core signals: views, likes, shares and comments
  • An overall engagement rate, plus per-post averages
  • Reach distribution across global markets
  • A clean, three-page report built for a top-down read

Key Insights

What the data tells us

The most important, decision-relevant takeaways — explained plainly.

1

Reach is driven by volume, not virality

Average views per post stay flat — around 2.5 million — across every platform, format and hashtag. Total reach is mostly a function of how much a channel posts.

2

Instagram earns the strongest engagement

At a 13.8% engagement rate, Instagram turns views into likes, shares and comments more efficiently than any other platform.

3

The USA is the largest audience

The United States accounts for 14.1% of all views — the biggest single market — followed by Brazil, the UK and Canada.

4

The "engagement level" label is misleading

Posts tagged "High" average slightly fewer views than those tagged "Low", so the label shouldn't be used to prioritise content.

5

Content format barely changes reach

Reels, Tweets, Videos, Shorts, Live Streams and Posts all land within a couple of percent of each other on average views.

6

Likes dominate the engagement mix

On every platform, likes make up roughly three-quarters of total engagement, with shares and comments making up the rest.
